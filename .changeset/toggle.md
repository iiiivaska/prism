Toggle (`spec/components/Toggle.yaml` 1): `<Toggle>` on the web and `DSToggle` in SwiftUI, a binary switch that takes effect the moment it is flipped, the control of a settings row. `isOn` is the value and `onChange` fires once with the new one (in SwiftUI, `isOn` is a `Binding<Bool>` that one flip writes once); `isDisabled` dims the row and takes it out of input and the focus order. The whole row is the target: a tap anywhere in it, label included, flips the switch, Space flips it and Enter does not, and a horizontal drag that starts on the track and moves 10 pt carries the knob and commits the nearest half on release, so a drag that ends where it began, or that crosses the middle and comes back, changes nothing. Off is an outlined track at the hairline with a quiet knob at the leading end; on is the inverse solid with the opposite-coloured knob at the trailing end, the white solid on vivid, and knocked out in the material's own foreground on inverse and accent. Under right to left the switch mirrors with the row. The row is the label, which leads and wraps, and the track, which trails and stays level with the label's first line. `label` is the switch's name, drawn unless `labelVisibility` is `hidden`, which keeps it as the name and draws the track alone; a Toggle with no name is reported in development. Hover, under pointer only, and a press lay one wash over the row, the material's own on inverse and accent. In SwiftUI the switch's accessibility element is SwiftUI's own `Toggle`, and one flip plays one haptic, `haptic.selection.on` or `haptic.selection.off`. The web package also exports `labelVisibilities` and the `LabelVisibility` type, which every Prism control that can hide its label will share, and SwiftUI adds `DSLabelVisibility`.
