Avatar (`spec/components/Avatar.yaml` 1): `<Avatar>` on the web and `DSAvatar` in SwiftUI, a circular portrait of a person, a vehicle or a site that is not a control: `name`, `image`, `size` `sm`, `md` or `lg` (the control height of that size, so it lines up with the buttons beside it), `hasRing` for the one active avatar, and `isDecorative`. It draws the image when there is one, the initials of `name` otherwise, upper-cased in the app's locale (`"Анна Петрова"` gives `"АП"`, Turkish `"i"` gives `"İ"`), and the `object.user` glyph when there is no name either. On the web `image` is a source that falls back to the initials when it fails to load and fades in once it has decoded; on Apple it is an already loaded `Image`, nil while it loads and after it fails, and it fades in when it arrives. Over a map, an image, a vivid surface or glass the circle is the Surface module's glass chip, its initials and ring switch to the on-glass colours, and under Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast it falls back to the raised surface with the standard colours. An avatar with a `name` is one image named by it, byte for byte on both stacks; one with no name, or marked `isDecorative`, is hidden, and no word is invented for the glyph. The web package also exports `avatarSizes` and its type.
