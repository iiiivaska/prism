Prism Direction BoardVerst, a cycling companion for an invented city

The city is the instrument panel. Glass follows the scheme: daylight rides sit on a paper map under light glass, night rides under neutral smoked glass. The report sets the loop in a framed plate beside a ledger of hairline charts and one thin hero numeral. One orange means “look here”, and on every screen it marks the same object: the climb ahead, or the climb ridden 2.1 km/h slower than last week.

03  What the board found

Tokens that looked wrong or weak once real screens were built from them. The three candidate boards found F1–F30 and three judges confirmed them; the final check of the first board found F31. P1-9 answered them: the owner’s decisions in ADR-0029, the technical findings by their proposals in ADR-0030, each outcome recorded below. What stays open is geometry and a recipe that later tickets pin.

Why the light screens changedD1 · F1, F2, F3, F4, F6, F7

Before P1-9, Card glass was smoked glass in the light scheme too. A daylight map is far brighter than the backdrop smoked glass is allowed over, so the app had to lay a scrim first, which greyed half the stage; the card read as a khaki slab, its white chart plot was a hole, and the white pause disc had no token. The phone on the left draws that recipe with today’s tokens: material.glass.dark.fill, whose smoke is neutral since F3, under the 0.45 scrim of F2. D1 made Card glass follow the scheme instead, and the light screens in 02 draw material.glass.fill: light glass with ink, no scrim.

Before, slots 1–4 mixed temperatures, and in dark ember-night’s orange end sat in the grid beside the one accent. Now each 2×2 alternates one slot pair, and ember-night is outside the slots (01, the bloom row). The before tiles use today’s gradients, so the brighter light set and the smoother navy-cyan show on both sides.
