02  Screens

Three screens of one product at device size, each in both schemes. Maps are drawn procedurally from a seed; every street, name and number is invented. Reference families are named by their shot ids in docs/research/references.json. The frames are pictures: their controls are inert, and the notes under each row describe them.

Live ride, the city under the rider

The stage is the map; one readout floats at the thumb, and the controls float over the world.

  • §4.3 · F1Card glass over the map. Light: glass.light.fill with text.primary, text.secondary and the dimmed “.4” (the proposal), no scrim. Dark: glass.dark.fill with its 1 px edge, elevation.3 and on-glass 100 / 78 / 64 %.
  • §4.16Map HUD. Round buttons and chips follow the scheme: glass.light.chip with ink in light, the smoked glass.dark.chip in dark. The turn cue is glass.light.fill, the selected object; the Cyrillic cue wraps instead of truncating.
  • §3.3 · F30Route under glass. On the dark phone the turn cue crosses the dashed route and the ride card covers the ridden route, so locally the backdrop is brighter than §3.3 allows: L 0.54 under the light cue (limit 0.35) and L 1.0 under the dark card (limit 0.67). Both hold at p95 (L 0.27), and the text keeps at least 4.89:1 on the cue and 7.8:1 on the card. The board tolerates thin lines under blur; F30 asks the map tokens to state it.
  • P2 · P9Pill grammar, one solid per group. The active segment is bg.fill.inverse; on the card the pause disc is the one solid: ink on light glass, white on dark glass.
  • §4.2Instrument numeral. metric.xl, tabular because it is live, with the dimmed trailing digit and the unit hung in metric.unit.
  • §4.12Datasheet ruler. Progress along the loop: elapsed ticks bright, the climb window in bg.tint.accent between two accent edges, the planned route dashed on the map.
  • P3One accent. The climb ahead: the route segment, the chip dot, the ruler window and the profile in the aside.
  • ADR-0022The fallback of the signature screen, as under Reduce Transparency: every glass surface is raised painted over bg.page with the standard tones, and the selected climb chip renders inverse (§1.6). The map stays. Increase Contrast uses the same fallback with its own tones, which a nested frame cannot show: data-ds-contrast is read from the root only, as modality is (F28).
  • ADR-0019Explicit axes. Each frame pins data-ds-color-scheme and data-ds-density="regular", so a touch viewer sees the same layout.

Families mixed: traffic console 27289370 (glass readout, instrument numeral), bottle tracker 27699907 (capsule chips over imagery), health tracker 27706847 (datasheet ruler), finance monitor 27696584 (one solid among hairlines). Distance: no reference phone floats a full-width readout under a turn cue; they pair a small offset card with a solid sheet.

03  What the board found

Tokens that looked wrong or weak once real screens were built from them, each with the exact token and the change proposed. The three candidate boards found F1–F30 and three judges confirmed them; the final check of this board found F31. These are the reason the board exists.

Why the light screens changedF1, F2, F3, F4, F6, F7

As generated, Card glass is smoked glass in the light scheme too. A daylight map is far brighter than the backdrop it is allowed over, so the app must lay a scrim first, which greys half the stage and still fails 3:1; the card reads as a khaki slab, its white chart plot is a hole, and the white pause disc has no token. The report card below shows the same failure. The screens in 02 draw the proposal instead, from existing tokens only.
