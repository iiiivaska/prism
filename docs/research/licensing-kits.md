# Prism — Legal posture and structural checklists (licensing, Dribbble, copyright, Figma kits)

Research date: 2026-09-08. Every claim carries a source URL and a confidence mark (H / M / L). "Verified" means the page was fetched or opened in a browser on 2026-09-08; "snippet" means the fact comes from a search-engine excerpt only. This is research, not legal advice.

---

## 0. Executive summary

1. **The studio behind the reference shots is RonDesignLab (rondesignlab.com, Kraków, Poland), not Ronas IT.** Ronas IT (ronasit.com, Tallinn, Estonia, since 2007) is a different studio with its own Dribbble team `@ronasit`. All nine shot IDs in the brief belong to the Dribbble team `RonDesignLab ⭐️` (eight verified directly in a browser; one — 27571204 — attributed via search snippets because Dribbble rate-limited the session).
2. **RonDesignLab sells no UI kits, templates or Figma files anywhere we could find** — not on their site, not on Figma Community, Gumroad or UI8, and their two Dribbble "Services" are hire listings (a $10 "Good Job!" card and a "My Precious" listing from $5,000,000). Their shots are marketing case studies with a "Get in touch" CTA and no download links. The only legitimate route to sources is to commission them (Clutch: minimum project $25,000+, $150–199/h).
3. **Dribbble's Terms (updated 17 March 2025) grant other users only a licence to *access* a shot through the Service**; copying/scraping and "accessing any User Content … through any technology or means other than those provided or authorized" are prohibited. Copyright stays with the member.
4. **Copyright protects specific expression, not ideas**: layouts, colour logic, typographic hierarchy, "glass over a map", "one card per metric" are unprotectable ideas/methods in the US (17 U.S.C. §102(b); Circular 33; 37 CFR 202.1) and in the EU protection needs the author's own intellectual creation (BSA C-393/09; Cofemel C-683/17). But the **exact composition, illustrations, icons, photos, copy and brand marks** are protectable, and since 1 May 2025 the EU design regime explicitly covers GUIs and animations, with an automatic 3-year unregistered design right against copying. RonDesignLab is EU-based.
5. **Figma Community free files are CC BY 4.0** (attribution required) — verified in-browser for SDS, Material 3, Untitled UI free, Primer Web, Polaris UI Kit, Radix Themes (unofficial), Obra shadcn CE and three glass/dashboard kits. **Exceptions:** Apple's kits are under Apple's own EULA (mock-ups for Apple platforms only, no extraction, no derivative works); Atlassian's ADS libraries are licensed only for Atlassian-integrated add-ons; Untitled UI's own licence forbids use in a redistributable UI kit/design system even though the Figma page shows CC BY 4.0; Matt Wierzbicki's shadcn kit is under the Community Paid Resource License.
6. **For Prism**: use the kits as *structural checklists* (component inventories, state matrices, density/variant axes), keep a machine-readable provenance/attribution file, and put an "inspiration firewall" between the RonDesignLab shots and Prism's actual assets.

## 3. Copyright basics — inspiration vs copying (US / EU)

### 3.1 United States

| Rule | Source (verbatim where quoted) | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Ideas are not protected: "In no case does copyright protection for an original work of authorship extend to any idea, procedure, process, system, method of operation, concept, principle, or discovery, regardless of the form in which it is described, explained, illustrated, or embodied in such work." | 17 U.S.C. §102(b) — https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/102 | H |
| Not copyrightable: "Words and short phrases such as names, titles, and slogans; familiar symbols or designs; mere variations of typographic ornamentation, lettering or coloring; mere listing of ingredients or contents" (§202.1(a)); "Typeface as typeface" (§202.1(e)). | 37 CFR §202.1 — https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/37/202.1 | H |
| Circular 33 "Works Not Protected by Copyright" (rev. March 2021) lists: ideas, methods, or systems; typeface, fonts and lettering; typographic ornamentation; colouring; familiar symbols and designs; "the layout or format" / arrangement; blank forms. | https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf | H |
| GUIs: "graphical user interfaces generated by computer programs are partly artistic and partly functional"; courts apply "the relevant limiting doctrines" (originality, functionality, standardization, scènes à faire, merger); after filtering unprotectable elements, "only 'thin' protection, against virtually identical copying, is appropriate" for Apple's desktop metaphor. | Apple Computer v. Microsoft, 35 F.3d 1435 (9th Cir. 1994) — https://law.resource.org/pub/us/case/reporter/F3/035/35.F3d.1435.93-16883.93-16869.93-16867.html | H |
| "Look and feel" as such is not copyrightable; trade dress (Lanham Act) can protect a distinctive, non-functional overall appearance with secondary meaning; design patents can protect ornamental GUI/icon designs. | Lexology/C&C IP overview — https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=1290012b-8072-48e8-bcec-e4826ff9c654 ; https://www.candcip.com/single-post/legal-protection-of-user-interface-and-user-experience-design-comparing-trade-dress-copyright-and | M (secondary) |

### 3.2 European Union

| Rule | Source | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| **BSA v Ministerstvo kultury, C-393/09 (22 Dec 2010):** a GUI "is not a form of expression of a computer program" under Directive 91/250 and cannot be protected as a program; it **can** be protected under Directive 2001/29 "if that interface is its author's own intellectual creation". The national court must take account of "the specific arrangement or configuration of all the components" of the interface; the originality criterion "cannot be met by components of the graphic user interface which are differentiated only by their technical function". | https://ipcuria.eu/case?reference=C-393%2F09 (fetched) ; EUR-Lex https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A62009CJ0393 ; Kluwer commentary https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/copyright-blog/protection-of-guis-graphical-user-interfaces-some-comments-about-the-ecj-s-preliminary-ruling-in-bsa-v-ministervo-kultury/ | H |
| **Cofemel, C-683/17 (12 Sept 2019):** originality is the sole requirement for copyright in designs; a work must be "an intellectual creation of its author" reflecting "free and creative choices" and be "identifiable with sufficient precision and objectivity"; where "realisation of an object is determined by technical considerations, rules or other constraints" there is no originality; aesthetic effect "does not in itself determine" protection. | https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/EU-copyright-in-designs-CJEU-rule-in-Cofemel-that-originality-is-the-only-requirement-for-protection | H |
| **Design right reform, Regulation (EU) 2024/2822** (OJ 18 Nov 2024; main provisions apply from **1 May 2025**, representation/animation filing rules from **1 July 2026**): "design" now includes "movement, transition or any other sort of animation" of features; "product" explicitly includes "graphical user interfaces", graphic works/symbols/logos, typefaces; product may be "embodied in a physical object or materialise[] in a non-physical form". **Unregistered EU design**: automatic protection for 3 years from first disclosure in the EU, against copying only. | EUR-Lex PDF https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX%3A32024R2822 ; IPKat https://ipkitten.blogspot.com/2024/11/design-reform-reaches-its-finale-it-is.html ; Dreyfus guide https://www.dreyfus.fr/en/2026/01/05/eu-design-protection-complete-guide-to-the-2025-reform/ ; HSF Kramer https://www.hsfkramer.com/notes/ip/2025-05/eu-design-reform-begins-key-provisions-of-eu-regulation-2024-2822-effective-from-1-may | H (definitions/dates) / M (applicability to Dribbble disclosure) |

### 3.3 Practical map: what is / is not protectable in the RonDesignLab references

| Not protectable (ideas, methods, styles) — free to be *inspired by* | Protectable — do not copy |
|---|---|
| "One card per metric", KPI grids, tab-per-category, dashboard-with-map layout ideas | The exact composition of a given screen reproduced near-verbatim (thin copyright; EU design right for 3 years if disclosed in the EU) |
| Colour *logic*: dark ops surfaces + orange accent; light airy gradients | Specific illustrations, 3D renders, mascots, photographs, maps imagery, custom icon sets |
| Typographic hierarchy: thin large numerals over small labels; a "geometric rounded sans" *category* | A specific typeface's font software (font files are protected as software even though the letterforms are not, in the US); bundled/embedded fonts need their own licence |
| Glass/blur over imagery as a material concept; elevation levels | Copy text, microcopy, case-study wording; the studio's name/logo (trademark) |
| Interaction patterns (swipe, tabs, filters), information architecture | Client brand names and logos (Hydro Flask, Credit Karma/Intuit, The Credit Pros) — trademark, never in Prism samples |
| Common UI conventions (scènes à faire), standard control shapes | Animations/transitions as *design* subject-matter in the EU since 1 May 2025 |

### 4.3 Apple Design Resources EULA — verbatim (PDF "LYL142 06/21/2023", the same document linked from the Figma page)

Source: https://developer.apple.com/support/downloads/terms/apple-design-resources/Apple-Design-Resources-License-20230621-English.pdf (read in full). Conf. H.

- "THE APPLE DESIGN RESOURCES LICENSED HEREUNDER ARE TO BE USED SOLELY FOR CREATING MOCK-UPS OF USER INTERFACES DESIGNED FOR USE IN SOFTWARE PRODUCTS THAT RUN ONLY ON APPLE'S macOS, iOS, watchOS, tvOS, AND/OR visionOS OPERATING SYSTEM SOFTWARE."
- §2A: "Subject to the terms of this License and your compliance with Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, you are granted a limited, non-transferable, non-exclusive license to use the Apple Design Resources solely for creating mock-ups of user interfaces designed for use in software products that run only on Apple's … operating system software ('Mock-Ups'). The foregoing right includes the right to show the Apple Design Resources in screen shots, images or other depictions of such Mock-Ups."
- §2B: "… you agree not to, install, use or run the Apple Design Resources for the purpose of creating mock-ups of user interfaces to be used in software products running on any non-Apple operating system software. You may not embed the Apple Design Resources in any software programs or other products. Except as expressly provided for herein, you may not use the Apple Design Resources to create, develop, display or otherwise distribute any documentation, artwork, website content or any other work product."
- §2C Template Content: "The Template Content may not otherwise be used, extracted, copied, modified, distributed, or repackaged as content, clip art, stock animation, or similar assets, or in any other manner." Recipients of Mock-Ups must be made "aware of the restrictions".
- §2D: no "modify, create derivative works of the Apple Design Resources or any part thereof".
- §1B: "The Apple San Francisco font is subject to a separate license agreement."

Related Apple licences:
- SF fonts (https://developer.apple.com/fonts/, EA1370/EA1371 dated 2/24/2016): "you may use the Apple Font solely for creating mock-ups of user interfaces to be used in software products running on Apple's iOS, OS X or tvOS operating systems"; "You may not embed the Apple Font in any software programs or other products"; not for mock-ups for non-Apple OS. (H)

(M — snippet, consistent with the HIG text)

## 5. What we may do / must not do (with sources)

### May do
1. **View** RonDesignLab shots on dribbble.com through the normal UI and take written notes of principles (Dribbble ToS §9(2), §7; copyright idea/expression rule 17 U.S.C. §102(b); BSA C-393/09).
2. **Be inspired by unprotectable elements**: layout ideas, colour logic, typographic hierarchy, material concepts, interaction patterns (Circular 33; 37 CFR 202.1; Apple v. Microsoft "thin" protection; BSA "technical function" components; Cofemel "technical considerations, rules or other constraints").
3. **Use CC BY 4.0 Figma files** (SDS, Material 3, Primer Web, Polaris UI Kit, Radix Themes community, Obra shadcn CE, the three glass kits) as inventories and even adapt them — **with attribution** to the creator in a NOTICE/attribution file (Figma help article; CC BY 4.0).
4. **Use Apple's kits to design Apple-platform mock-ups** for Prism's iOS/iPadOS/macOS/watchOS surfaces and show those mock-ups in screenshots (Apple EULA §2A), and read them as the canonical control/state inventory.
5. **Use open-source code libraries as behaviour references**: shadcn/ui, Radix Themes, Polaris, Primer (MIT), Material Web and Adobe spectrum-design-data (Apache-2.0) — including adapting Spectrum's component-schema/token structure with the Apache NOTICE preserved.
6. **Ship Inter (OFL 1.1) and Phosphor (MIT)** in Prism with their licence texts.
7. **Commission RonDesignLab** if actual sources or a concept sprint are wanted (site contact; Dribbble "Get in touch"/Services; expect ≥ $25k per Clutch).

### Must not do
1. **Scrape, bulk-download, or access Dribbble by automated means or by bypassing its bot/rate-limit measures** (ToS §7). Do not build a Dribbble ingestion tool for the AI agent.
2. **Reproduce a RonDesignLab screen** (or its illustrations, icons, photos, copy) in Prism's Figma library, docs, sample apps or repo; do not trace or "recreate" it pixel-for-pixel (copyright in the specific expression; EU unregistered design right for 3 years from disclosure under Regulation 2024/2822; Dribbble DMCA policy).
3. **Use third-party brand names/logos** (Hydro Flask, Credit Karma/Intuit, The Credit Pros, Soma, Arvion, Vexto, RonDesignLab) in Prism demos or fixtures (trademark; rondesignlab.com Terms of Use "Marks").
4. **Derive Prism's web/React kit, Tailwind theme, tokens or Figma library from Apple's Design Resources**, extract Template Content, embed them in software, or use them for non-Apple mock-ups (Apple EULA §2B–2D). Do not ship SF fonts or SF Symbols on the web (SF font licence; HIG).
5. **Use Atlassian ADS components/assets** in Prism at all (atlassian.design/license: Atlassian-integrated add-ons only, no derivatives).
6. **Copy Untitled UI components/assets** into Prism (creator licence forbids use "in any form of UI kit, design system, code library, template" for distribution, despite the CC BY 4.0 badge). Inventory-only.
7. **Copy from paid Community files** (e.g., Matt Wierzbicki's shadcn kit — Community Paid Resource License: no copy/modify/derivative works) without purchasing and honouring the vendor licence.
8. **Publish anything derived from a CC BY 4.0 file without attribution** (CC BY 4.0 §3(a)).
9. **Copy the Dribbble website's own UI/assets** (ToS §11).
