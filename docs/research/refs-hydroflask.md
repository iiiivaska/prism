**Family:** hydroflask — consumer hydration mobile with a responsive daily gradient
**Sources**
- Set A — shot 27699907 "Hydroflask – Smart Water Bottle Mobile App" (RonDesignLab): a/00–a/07 jpg + a/09 video still
- Set B — shot 27619760, same project, earlier post (Jack R. for RonDesignLab): b/00, b/02–b/07 jpg (b/01 is byte-identical to b/00) + b/09 video still

**Method.** Every jpg/png viewed at full 2000 px; colours sampled with ffmpeg as 13×13 px area averages (≈120 samples); sizes calibrated on a/06 (screen ≈1160 px tall for 852 pt → ≈1.37 px/pt) and cross-checked against the 17 pt status-bar clock in b/06. All pt values are for a 393 pt-wide iPhone and are ±10 %. Photographic mockups (tilt, shallow depth of field, studio lighting) darken and warm the samples slightly; where a flat render exists (a/00, a/02) it was preferred.

**Off-family frames.** a/09.png (SugarCRM "Cases Overview" web dashboard) and b/09.png (Navexa warehouse dashboard) are RonDesignLab promo-video stills, not hydroflask screens. Noted in the appendix for house-style context only.

## 1. Overview

Two screens of one product, presented in Ronas IT's "macro photography" way (tilted phone, shallow DOF, hands and figures as black silhouettes, film grain over everything):

1. **Hydrogen & Water Stats** (set A) — a light, cool-grey screen. Header with two hairline round buttons and a centred title; a three-chip segmented control (solid white active chip); one large **dark-glass chart card** ("Flasks Per Day" bell curve over a navy → cyan gradient with a purple bloom); a 2×2 grid of **vivid gradient capsule tiles** (Flasks/Day, Mol. Hydrogen, Water) plus one **clear-glass "+" tile**.
2. **How much did you drink today?** (set B) — the log screen. The **entire background is the daily gradient** (orange → hot magenta → coral → sand → pale yellow), a 60 pt **dot-matrix numeral stepper** ("16 Ounces", hairline − / + circles), a section "Or Choose An Amount" with three **frosted-glass preset rows** (Glass 8 oz, Mug 12 oz, Bottle 16 oz) containing 3D vessel renders inside a dotted "specimen ring" and a small glass label pill, each with a large round glass "+" button, and a **floating glass tab bar** with a solid white active circle.

The concept statement — "one core number, a few tactile presets, a responsive daily gradient that mirrors progress in real time" — is visible in every frame: the colour field *is* the progress indicator, the numeral is the only large typographic element, and everything else is hairline-thin, Regular-weight, and semi-transparent.

Compared with the owner's hand-picked screenshots (light CRM, dark Vexto ops), this family shares the RonDesignLab DNA — capsule chips with a solid active pill, hairline circular icon buttons, ↗ open-in-circle, dotted reference lines, vivid gradient tiles with a light edge, hero numerals with dimmed units — but pushes three things further: (a) **glass everywhere, tinted only by what is behind it**; (b) **film grain + feathered edges + colour-bleed glow instead of drop shadows**; (c) a **dot-matrix LED numeral** as the brand's display device, echoing the smart bottle's own display. The text face is a neo-grotesque (SF Pro), not the geometric rounded sans of the CRM shots.

**Mood:** mixed — light page chrome, a dark glass hero, and full-bleed vivid gradients.

## 4. Surfaces & Depth

There are exactly four surface materials, and **no drop shadows at all**. Depth comes from blur, glow, grain and opacity.

| Material | Where | Fill | Edge | Blur | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solid light** | active chip, "+" tile on light page | #F8F8F8 | none (the value contrast is the edge) | — | very faint ambient shadow (≈ 2 % black, 8 pt) or none |
| **Light glass** | inactive chips, ± circles, preset rows, "+" circles, tab bar, label pills, delta pills | white 25–45 % (chips 25, rows 30, tab bar 45, pills 35–40) | 1 px white 50–60 % specular edge (top-left brighter) | backdrop ≈ 20–30 px | inner 1 px **dotted seam** inset 4–6 pt (rows, tiles); pills refract the image beneath (Liquid-Glass distortion) |
| **Dark glass** | chart card, "+" tile over dark imagery | navy #1E2A3A at ≈ 80 % + gradient to cyan | none — **feathered edge** ≈ 3–6 px | backdrop ≈ 30 px | two radial colour blooms (mauve right, cyan bottom); grain ≈ 10 %; faint light halo |
| **Vivid gradient** | stat tiles | 3-stop linear gradient (see Color) | none — feathered ≈ 2–3 px | — | grain ≈ 10 %; inner dotted seam; colour-bleed glow ≈ 25 % / 30 pt onto the page |

Other depth devices:
- **Lens marker** (chart): ≈ 28 pt disc, fill white 10 %, 1 px rim with a chromatic (rainbow) fringe, bright top-left specular, 3 pt solid white centre dot. Reads as a physical glass bead lying on the line.
- **3D renders** (glass, mug, bottle) are frosted/transparent objects with condensation droplets — they inherit the gradient's colour; the label pill sits on top of them and refracts them.
- **Grain** is global (≈ 8–12 % monochrome noise, overlay blend), strongest on the dark card and the vivid tiles; it is what stops the gradients from looking like plastic.
- Radii: circles (28 / 44–48 / 56 / 84 pt), capsules (radius = 50 % of height: chips 42 pt, pills 22 pt, rows 95 pt, tab bar 72 pt), stat tiles (radius ≈ 48 % of height ≈ 48 pt), one large squircle card (radius ≈ 40 pt on 366 pt width ≈ 11 %).

## 5. Typography

**Family (text):** SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text — a neo-grotesque. Evidence: single-storey "g" in "Hydrogen", straight-tailed "y" in "Day"/"today", the "?" bowl, the "&", the "R"-less but Helvetica-like "k" and "s", ellipsis truncation "Molecular H…", and the status-bar clock is the system 9:41. It is *not* Poppins/Outfit/Urbanist (no geometric round "o", double-storey "a" present) and not Manrope (no squarish curves). Helvetica Neue is the closest non-Apple match; Inter is the practical web stand-in.

**Weight:** Regular (400) everywhere — titles, labels, captions. The only heavier glyphs are the system clock (Semibold) and the active-day letter "W" (reads Medium because it is dark on white). Hierarchy is achieved by **size and opacity**, never by weight or colour.

**Family (numerals):** a **dot-matrix / LED display face** — 4×7 round-dot grid per glyph (the "1" carries a two-dot diagonal flag; the "6" and "8" are the classic rounded LED forms; the decimal point is a single dot). Used for every quantitative number (hero 16, tile 16.2 / 2.8 / 76, preset 8 / 12 / 16). Looks like a custom cut or a font such as "Dot Matrix"/"LED Dot-Matrix" with round dots. Digits are tabular by construction.

**Observed sizes** (pt on a 393 pt device ≈ CSS px at 1×):
| Element | Size | Weight / colour | Opacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero dot-matrix numeral ("16") | **≈ 60** (7 rows × 6 pt dot + 2.5 pt gap) | white | 100 % |
| Screen title ("How much did you drink today?") | **≈ 32**, leading ≈ 1.1 | Regular, white | 100 % |
| Card title ("Flasks Per Day") | **≈ 20** | Regular, white | 100 % |
| Nav title / section title ("Hydrogen & Water Stats", "Or Choose An Amount") | **17** | Regular, ink / white | 100 % |
| Chip label | **15** | Regular; ink (active) / grey #7A8082 (inactive) | — |
| Tile numeral (dot-matrix "16.2", "2.8") | **≈ 18–20** | white | 100 %; decimals dimmed ≈ 60 % |
| Preset numeral ("8", "12") | **≈ 20** | white | 100 % |
| Date pager, subtitle, "Ounces" | **13** | Regular, white | 100 % (pager) / 55 % (subtitle, Ounces) |
| Tile label ("Flasks/Day", "Mol. Hydrogen", "Water"), label pills ("Glass") | **12** | Regular, white | 100 % |
| Delta pill text, day letters (S M T W T F S) | **10–11** | Regular, white | 100 % (pill) / 55 % (days) |
| Units ("mg", "oz") | **10** | Regular, white | 60 % |

**Casing & tracking:** Title Case for everything including captions ("Log Additional Water Consumed Outside Of Your Flask-Tap…") — an unusual, slightly editorial choice; tracking is default (no letter-spacing tricks); no uppercase labels anywhere.

**Numerals style:** thin-and-airy display numerals are replaced by the dot-matrix device; units are small (10 pt), 60 % opacity, baseline-aligned right of the number with ≈ 4 pt gap; decimals are visibly dimmer; digits carry **motion-blur trails** in every still (mid-roll), a strong hint that numbers animate by vertical roll.

## 6. Layout & Spacing

- **Page margins:** 16 pt (set A), ≈ 20 pt (set B).
- **Vertical rhythm (set A):** status bar → 8 → header row 48 → 16 → chip row 42 → 16 → chart card 336 → 16 → tile grid (2 × 102 with 14 gap) → 24 to the home indicator. One card + four tiles fill the viewport exactly; no scrolling implied.
- **Vertical rhythm (set B):** status → 24 → title (2 × 35) → 12 → subtitle (2 × 17) → 40 → stepper row 60 → 12 → "Ounces" → 40 → section title → 16 → preset rows (95 + 12 + 95 + …) → floating tab bar 72 tall, 24 from the bottom, 16 side margins. The tab bar overlaps the third row (content scrolls under it).
- **Grid:** 2 columns for tiles with a 14 pt gutter (tiles ≈ 168 pt); a single 366 pt column for the card; preset rows are [row capsule ≈ 250 pt][12 pt][round button 84 pt].
- **Card padding:** chart card 22–24 pt (title baseline at ≈ 40 pt from the top; ↗ circle 20 pt from top/right); tiles 14 pt; tab bar 16 pt.
- **Component gaps:** chips 8 pt; stepper ± circles 40 pt from the numeral; header buttons flush with the 16 pt margin.
- **Alignment:** nav title centred; card title left; tile label centred, numeral bottom-left, delta pill bottom-right; hero numeral centred; preset numeral left-aligned in its own 64 pt column.
- **Density:** deliberately low — 1 chart, 3 numbers, 1 CTA per screen; no lists, no tables, no secondary charts.

## 9. Iconography

- **Style:** linear, single 1.5 px stroke (1.25 px inside 10-pt pills), rounded caps and joins, no fills except the solid active tab circle's glyph (which is still an outline drop, in black).
- **Vocabulary seen:** ← back, ⓘ info, ↗ open/expand, ‹ › pager chevrons, + add, − decrement, ↑ ↓ delta arrows, ○ water drop (tab, active), ⁘ four rounded squares "presets/grid" (tab), a notes/book rectangle with a dot (tab), the system battery/clock.
- **Sizing:** glyphs ≈ 18 pt inside 46–48 pt circles, 20–22 pt in the tab bar, 8–9 pt inside delta pills.
- **Colour:** ink #1A1A1A on light surfaces; white on dark/vivid; icons never coloured.
- **Containment:** almost every icon lives inside a hairline circle — the circle is the button, the icon is small and centred; the exception is the tab bar where inactive icons float bare on the glass.
- **Family guess:** close to SF Symbols "ultralight/thin" or a Feather/Phosphor-Thin equivalent on web; the drop and grid glyphs are custom.

## 10. What makes it beautiful (concrete, numbered)

1. **One weight, one family, no coloured text.** Every glyph is SF Pro Regular in white or near-black; hierarchy comes from 10 / 13 / 17 / 20 / 32 / 60 pt sizes and 100 / 55 % opacity. Because type never competes, the gradients read as the emotional layer.
2. **Colour lives in surfaces, not in elements.** The page is a neutral #E3E7E9; every drop of saturation is inside a capsule or the full-bleed field — so saturation feels intentional and composed instead of scattered.
3. **No drop shadows; depth by light.** Feathered 3–6 px edges, blur, film grain ≈ 10 % and a 25 % colour-bleed halo replace shadows. Surfaces look like light sources sitting on the page instead of cards stacked on it.
4. **A two-primitive shape system.** Circles (28 / 48 / 56 / 84 pt) and capsules (radius = 50 % height) for everything, plus a single 40 pt-radius squircle hero card. Chips, tiles, rows, tab bar and pills all rhyme.
5. **The hairline "technical" layer.** 1 px rings, 1 px dotted seams inset 5 pt in every card, dotted reference lines, dotted drop lines — a consistent thin graphic grammar drawn over lush colour gives precision to softness.
6. **Dot-matrix numerals as the brand device.** The LED grid echoes the bottle's own display, makes digits tabular by construction, and the motion-blur trails make the still frames feel alive.
7. **Glass with no colour of its own.** The same 30 % white recipe reads pink over magenta, straw over yellow, near-black over a coat — the material is honest and the whole screen stays chromatically coherent.
8. **The gradient is the data.** Progress is felt before it is read; the number confirms it. This is "one core number" done literally.
9. **Temperature per screen.** Stats = cool navy → cyan; log = hot magenta → straw. Each screen has one temperature, so mixed screens never look muddy.
10. **Micro-refinements that cost effort:** the prismatic rim on the lens marker, refracting label pills over renders, dimmed decimals, the dirty olive bottom of the pink tile, the dotted "specimen ring" around each vessel.
11. **Radical low density.** A viewport holds one card plus four tiles, or one number plus three rows. Whitespace is the luxury.
12. **Photographic presentation.** Grain, shallow DOF, silhouette hands — not a product feature, but the reason the shots read as premium; Prism should reproduce the *in-app* grain and softness, not the mockup.

## 11. Accessibility risks

1. **55 % white captions on hot magenta/orange** ("Log Additional Water…", "Ounces", tile labels at 12 pt on pink) — estimated 2.0–2.8 : 1, fails AA for body-size text.
2. **10 pt units ("mg", "oz") at 60 % white** on gradients — below 3 : 1 and below minimum comfortable size.
3. **Day letters 10–11 pt at 55 % white** on the cyan part of the chart card — ≈ 2.5 : 1.
4. **Inactive chip text #7A8082 on glass ≈ #C9D0D0** — ≈ 2.9 : 1 at 15 pt; fails AA.
5. **Delta pill text 10.5 pt** on pink/orange glass — borderline 3 : 1; the pill ring is the only affordance.
6. **Dot-matrix numerals:** 4×7 grids at 18–20 pt are hard to decode ("6"/"8"/"0", "2"/"Z"); the motion-blur trails and dimmed decimals reduce legibility further; must never be the only representation for assistive tech or Larger Text.
7. **Contrast depends on state.** Because the background gradient changes with progress, text contrast is unpredictable; a hydration level that lands white text on the pale-yellow end (#DED9B6) drops to ≈ 1.4 : 1.
8. **Hairline-only buttons** (1 px ring at 40 % white) for − / + and nav actions are low-visibility, though 46–48 pt targets meet size requirements.
9. **Transparency and blur** must respect Reduce Transparency / prefers-reduced-transparency with a solid fallback; grain should drop under Increase Contrast.
10. **Colour-only progress encoding** (the daily gradient) needs the numeral and an accessible value/progress label; the chart has no axis values, so the series must be described (values per day) for screen readers.
11. **Truncated chip labels** ("Molecular H…") lose meaning; needs full accessibility labels and a wider chip or shorter copy.
12. **Motion:** rolling numerals with blur and gradient shifts must honour Reduce Motion (crossfade instead).

## 12. Principles to carry into Prism (numbered, with numbers)

1. **Type scale and weight.** One neo-grotesque (SF Pro on Apple, Inter on web), Regular 400 for all UI text; scale 10 / 11 / 12 / 13 / 15 / 17 / 20 / 32 pt; hero numerals 56–64 pt. Hierarchy by size and opacity only: primary 100 %, secondary 70 % (raise from the reference's 55 % for AA), tertiary 55 % only at ≥ 17 pt.
2. **Two text colours.** `ink` #141414 on light/solid surfaces; `on-vivid` #FFFFFF on vivid and dark glass. Coloured text is forbidden; direction/semantics go into glyphs (↑ ↓) and pills.
3. **Shape tokens.** `radius.circle`; `radius.capsule` = 50 % of height (min height 40 pt for touch chips, 22 pt for pills); `radius.tile` = 48 % of height; `radius.card` = 40 pt (≈ 11 % of a 360-pt card, clamp 28–44 pt); `radius.tabbar` = 50 %.
4. **Hairline tokens.** `hairline` 1 px: white 45 % on dark/vivid, ink 18 % on light; `hairline.dotted` 1 px with 2 px gap for seams and reference lines; `seam.inset` 5 pt inside tiles/rows.
5. **Surface materials (four).** `solid.light` #F8F8F8 on page #E3E7E9; `vivid` = 3-stop linear gradient at 165° with grain 8–10 % (overlay), feathered edge 2–3 px, colour-bleed glow (top stop at 25 %, 30 pt blur); `glass.light` = white 30 % + backdrop blur 24 px + 1 px white 55 % edge + inner dotted seam; `glass.dark` = #1E2A3A 80 % + blur 30 px + two radial blooms (12–16 % each) + grain 10 %. Glass only over imagery/maps/vivid (project rule); on plain light pages, chips and tab bars use `solid.light` or the system material.
6. **No drop shadows.** Elevation = { blur, glow, grain, opacity }. If a shadow is unavoidable on web, max black 4 % / 24 pt blur.
7. **Chart grammar.** Series: 1.5 px white solid for the focus range, 1 px dotted at 70 % elsewhere; max two dotted reference lines at 30 %; no gridlines, no y-axis labels on cards (values live in stat tiles); marker = 3 pt dot inside a 28 pt glass lens; category axis = 28 pt hairline circles with a solid active disc; a range pager (‹ label ›, 13 pt) below. Micro-sparklines in tiles: 1 px dotted arcs, ≤ 2 strokes.
8. **Numeral system.** `display.dot` — a round-dot 4×7 matrix face for hero and stat numbers (60 pt: dot 6 pt / gap 2.5 pt; 20 pt: dot 2 pt / gap 0.85 pt), tabular; a plain SF Pro Light numeral fallback for Larger Text/Reduce Motion and as the accessibility value. Units 11 pt at 60 %, baseline-aligned, 4 pt gap; decimals may dim to 60 % at ≥ 24 pt only.
9. **Pill family.** One 22-pt capsule component covers delta pills, image labels and micro-tags: white 35 % fill, 1 px 60 % ring, 10.5–12 pt text, optional 1.25 px arrow glyph.
10. **Layout rhythm.** Page margins 16 pt (20 pt for full-bleed screens); section gap 16 pt; chip gap 8 pt; tile gutter 14 pt; card padding 22 pt; tile padding 14 pt; floating tab bar 72 pt tall with 16 pt side margins and 24 pt bottom offset; content scrolls under it.
11. **Density cap.** One hero card + four tiles per viewport, or one hero number + three action rows. Anything more becomes a second screen.
12. **Icon tokens.** 1.5 px linear glyphs, rounded caps, 18 pt inside 46–48 pt hairline circles, 22 pt in tab bars; inactive icons bare, active icon inside a 56 pt solid white disc with an ink glyph.
13. **Responsive state gradient.** Token ramp `gradient.state.{0,25,50,75,100}` from hot (#D66038 → #E4286A → #DED9B6) to cool (#252E3F → #5DA8B9 → #9DC4CC); interpolate over 600–900 ms; each screen commits to one temperature at a time.
14. **Motion.** Numerals roll vertically with a 250–350 ms ease-out and a subtle motion-blur pass; lens marker follows drag on a spring (response 0.35, damping 0.8); glass edges pick up specular on tilt/scroll (Liquid Glass on Apple); Reduce Motion → crossfade.
15. **Accessibility guardrails.** Text on vivid/glass is contrast-checked against the *worst* stop of the gradient ramp and gets an automatic 12 % ink/white scrim when it fails; captions ≥ 12 pt at ≥ 70 %; units ≥ 11 pt; ring-only buttons get a 12 % fill on hover/focus and a 2 px focus ring; solid fallbacks for Reduce Transparency; every chart exposes per-point values; the state gradient is always paired with the numeral and an accessible progress label.

## Appendix A — Motion hints read from stills

- **Rolling digits:** every dot-matrix numeral shows downward motion-blur trails on its lower rows (b/00, b/04, b/06, a/03) → digits scroll vertically like an odometer/LED ticker; the blur suggests a fast tween (≈ 250–300 ms) with a blur pass rather than a crossfade.
- **Draggable lens:** the peak marker's glass lens with a chromatic rim (a/01, a/05) reads as a scrubber that magnifies the line; expect it to slide along the spline with the dotted drop line following.
- **Responsive gradient:** the concept copy and the cool/hot split between screens imply the background field animates with progress; b/03's top-right peach lobe vs b/06's magenta core suggests the mesh points move, not just a colour swap.
- **Glass responds to backdrop:** the clear "+" tile switching from white to dark with a cyan bloom (a/06 vs a/00) implies live backdrop sampling — on Apple this is native Liquid Glass; on web a backdrop-filter with a tinted overlay.
- **Sparkline "draw-on":** the hook at the end of the Flasks/Day dotted arc and the bead marker suggest the arc animates in and the delta pill lands last.
